Have questions about Computer Engineering? Our FAQ section covers everything you need to know about the discipline, skills, and career paths.

Computer Engineering is a discipline that integrates electronic engineering with computer science to design, build, and optimize hardware systems, software applications, embedded technologies, and digital architecture.

While Computer Science focuses heavily on algorithms, software development, and theoretical computing, and Electrical Engineering focuses on power circuits and signal processing, Computer Engineering sits right at the intersection—bridging hardware and software integration.

You will gain expertise in circuit design, microcontroller programming, logic system design, computer architecture, networking, object-oriented programming, operating systems, IoT systems, and embedded firmware development.

Graduates can work in diverse tech industries as Embedded Systems Engineers, Hardware Design Engineers, Network Architects, Software Engineers, Systems Analysts, Cybersecurity Consultants, and Robotics Specialists.

Prior experience is beneficial but not strictly necessary. Core programs build foundational knowledge starting from basic mathematics, logic, introductory electronics, and fundamental programming concepts.
