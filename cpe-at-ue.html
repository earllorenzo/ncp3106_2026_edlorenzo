CpE in UE - Manila

Bachelor of Science in Computer
Engineering

The BS Computer Engineering program at the University of the East combines hardware, software, mathematics, and engineering into a 4-year course. Preparing students to design and develop modern computing systems through hands-on learning and projects.

Program
Objectives

Discover the key learning outcomes and career objectives designed for Computer Engineering students.

01

Technical Expertise

Master fundamental and advanced principles in computer systems engineering, embedded hardware design, software development, and network architecture to solve complex real-world technical problems.

02

Professional Leadership

Demonstrate strong leadership, ethical integrity, effective communication, and collaborative teamwork while managing multi-disciplinary engineering projects in global enterprise environments.

03

Innovation & Research

Engage in groundbreaking research and technological innovation across artificial intelligence, Internet of Things (IoT), robotics, and cybersecurity to advance societal and industrial development.

04

Lifelong Learning

Adapt to evolving technological trends and maintain continuous professional development through graduate studies, specialized industry certifications, and independent technical mastery.

01

UE-Tan Yan Kee Foundation, Inc. Scholarship

For qualified incoming freshmen pursuing selected degree programs. Benefits include 100% free tuition and miscellaneous fees, book allowance, uniform subsidy, transportation allowance, and monthly stipend.

02

Athletic & Cultural Service Grants

For students with outstanding athletic or artistic abilities who qualify for the UE Pep Squad, UAAP-level competition, UE Band, UE Chorale, UE Drama Company, or UE Silanganan Dance Troupe.

03

University Scholarship

[100% free tuition and miscellaneous fees] — Awarded for one semester, renewal every semester, to UE undergraduate students with a grade point average (GPA) of 1.0000 to 1.2500.

04

College Scholarship

[50% free tuition and miscellaneous fees] — Awarded for one semester, renewal every semester, to UE undergraduate students with a Grade Point Average (GPA) of 1.2501 to 1.5000.
